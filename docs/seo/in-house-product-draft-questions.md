# Hoodie and T-shirt draft fact checklist

The local `/products/hoodies` and `/products/t-shirts` routes are deliberately `noindex`, excluded from the sitemap, and not linked from navigation or internal content. They use existing page components and inspected local product imagery, but must not be published until the following facts are approved.

| Fact to confirm | Hoodie draft | T-shirt draft | Evidence that resolves it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Styles, fits and size range | Required | Required | Approved product specification sheet |
| Fabric composition, GSM, colours and finishes | Required | Required | Approved material/specification sheet |
| MOQ by style, colour and decoration method | Required | Required | Current commercial policy with effective date |
| Sample process, timing and approval stages | Required | Required | Approved customer workflow |
| Suitable in-house decoration/finishing methods by garment | Required | Required | Capability matrix with constraints |
| Product photos permitted for public use | Required | Required | Owner-approved asset list |

When facts are confirmed, remove the bracketed owner markers, review each claim against the source evidence, decide whether a Product schema type accurately represents the page, then separately approve indexing, sitemap inclusion and internal links. Do not publish a generic placeholder page.
