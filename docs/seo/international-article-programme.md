### Official-source research already located

For later reviewed drafts, use official sources only: UK [import guide](https://www.gov.uk/import-goods-into-uk) and [trade tariff](https://www.gov.uk/trade-tariff); US [FTC apparel labelling](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/topics/tools-consumers/apparel-labeling) and [FTC care labelling](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/clothes-captioning-complying-care-labeling-rule); EU [textile-labelling FAQ](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/document/download/34fcf863-59ef-4352-8489-a2577102fd8f_en?filename=revised+FAQs+on+Regulation+1007-2011+-+published.pdf); Australia [Border Force import requirements](https://www.abf.gov.au/imports/Pages/How-to-import/Requirements.aspx); and Canada [CBSA commercial-import guide](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/import/guide-eng.html). Recheck every source at publication time, record the actual review date, and do not convert general legal guidance into an OSD commercial promise.
